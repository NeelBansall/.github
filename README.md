### **Project Overview**

**Aarogya Sahayak** is a user-friendly mobile health platform designed to empower individuals in India's underserved regions to effectively manage chronic illnesses like diabetes and hypertension. Our mission is to bridge the gap between patients and consistent medical guidance, thereby improving accessibility, adherence, and overall quality of life.

The app features a multilingual interface, enabling users to effortlessly log and visualize their daily vitals, including blood sugar, blood pressure, and weight. It provides personalized health advice, automated medication reminders, and a direct connection to local ASHA health workers, ensuring continuous and personalized care.

### **Key Features ✨**

* **Multilingual Interface:** The app supports multiple local languages, making it accessible to a wide range of users in India.

* **Vital Logging & Visualization:** Users can easily log and track their blood sugar, blood pressure, and weight. Simple, intuitive charts help them understand their health trends over time.

* **Personalized Health Advice:** The app provides actionable health tips and guidance tailored to the user's specific health data and condition.

* **Automated Reminders:** Users receive automated reminders for medications and upcoming check-ups, helping them stay on track with their treatment plans.

* **ASHA Worker Connection:** The platform facilitates direct communication between patients and local ASHA health workers, ensuring personalized and local support.

### **Tech Stack 🛠️**

* **Frontend:** **Flutter** - A versatile framework for building beautiful, natively compiled applications for mobile, web, and desktop from a single codebase.

* **Backend:** **FastAPI** - A modern, fast (high-performance) web framework for building APIs with Python 3.7+.

* **Database:** **PostgreSQL** - A powerful, open-source relational database system.

* **Notifications:** **OneSignal** - A comprehensive platform for push notifications, in-app messaging, and more.

* **Charts:** **Flutter Charts** - A charting library for Flutter to create dynamic and interactive data visualizations.

* **Deployment:**

  * **Frontend:** **Vercel**

  * **Backend:** **AWS/GCP** (or another cloud provider)
